The problem

A shipment can reach ABI before its paperwork is filing-ready.

Commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, and client emails arrive in different formats, at different times, from different people. Brokers reconcile them by hand, against a filing deadline so mismatches in quantity, value, or classification often surface after the entry is already submitted, when they're a CBP hold or a rejected entry instead of a two-minute fix.

How it works

Trade File Intelligence: what happens to every file you receive

This is Nort's core product the process that runs automatically on every shipment, from the moment a client's document lands to the moment you have a Golden Record ready to file.

01

Receive

Clients send documents however they already do upload or email. No new portal for them to learn.

02

Categorize

Each document is identified and sorted commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin into the right shipment.

03

Detect anomalies

Documents are cross-checked against each other for mismatches in quantity, value, or classification.

04

Notify

If something doesn't line up, you're told exactly which documents and fields are in conflict before you file, not after.

05

Consolidate

Everything verified comes together into one Golden Record your single source of truth for the shipment.
