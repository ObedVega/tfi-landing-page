The problem

A shipment does not arrive as one trusted record.

It arrives as invoices, packing lists, manifests, certificates, emails, spreadsheets, and conflicting versions of the same facts. Entry writers must reconstruct the shipment before they can begin entry work.

Without Nort

The shipment lives across disconnected documents.

Client email threads Commercial invoices Packing lists Certificates and manifests Spreadsheets Multiple document versions

The brokerage must determine what arrived, what is missing, where values conflict, and which source should be trusted.

→
With Nort

Every shipment has one controlled Trade File.

  • Documents remain connected to the shipment
  • Extracted values remain linked to source evidence
  • Conflicts stay visible until a human resolves them
  • Ownership and decisions become part of the record
The unit of work
