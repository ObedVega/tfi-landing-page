The problem

A shipment can move before its information is ready.

Purchase orders, invoices, packing lists, origin evidence, classifications, and broker requests are spread across companies, inboxes, PDFs, and spreadsheets. Problems are often discovered after the goods are already moving when they are harder and more expensive to fix.

How Kiger works

From supplier evidence to a shipment ready for handoff.

The workflow starts before the material ships and becomes more complete as each participant contributes evidence.

01

Create the shipment

The manufacturer or importer creates the shipment from a purchase order or internal reference.

02

Collect supplier evidence

The supplier uploads invoices, packing lists, origin evidence, and supporting product documents.

03

Build and review the record

Kiger extracts structured information, compares sources, and routes uncertain or conflicting fields for human review.

04

Track readiness and exceptions

The importer sees what is complete, what is missing, who must act, and whether the shipment is ready to move forward.

The Golden Record

Documents are evidence. The shipment is the record.

Kiger turns fragmented evidence into a living, auditable representation of the shipment. Each source keeps its provenance, each conflict remains visible, and every approved change becomes part of the record's history.
